FILAMENT REACTIONS

Some materials are chill.
Others are very dramatic.

PLA

Usually the least dramatic one in the room. It can tolerate more day-to-day variation, but better storage is still a smart habit.

PETG

More likely to absorb moisture over time. If the room stays humid, drying it before an important print is probably worth it.

TPU / Nylon

These are the divas. If the humidity hangs around too long, they will absolutely make it your problem later.
